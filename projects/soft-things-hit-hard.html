Overview

Soft Things Hit Hard is a third-person party mini-royale where squishy, Play-Doh-like creatures enter the arena to scavenge junk, attach makeshift protection, and assemble weapons. In a chaotic fight to become the last blob standing, creativity is your greatest weapon.
